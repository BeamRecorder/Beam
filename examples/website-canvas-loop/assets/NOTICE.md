# Frozen Beam library assets

The wallpaper images are downscaled derivatives of the same named WebP files
in `public/wallpapers/image/`. `wispysky.mp4` is a silent eight-second, 640-pixel
derivative of `public/wallpapers/video/wispysky.mp4`; the poster is its first frame.
Existing library assets retain their applicable rights. No new CC0 dedication,
ownership or independent upstream license is claimed for those assets.

`beautiful-captures.webp` comes from the user's Beam — Beautiful Captures project,
exported with Beam CLI. Its platform marks retain their rights.

`macos-pointer.svg` is copied unchanged from `public/macOsSvgCursors/default.svg`.
Its real pack descriptor/hotspot and Beam's native spring/ripple math are reused.
See `docs/agents/templates/assets/NOTICE.md` for existing cursor provenance.

Hanken Grotesk retains its supplied SIL OFL. Vue, GSAP, Lucide, vue-i18n and
Mediabunny retain their own licenses. The original native controls, background
presets/painter, Safari renderer and Bloom gradient are imported from Beam.

The HyperFrames press-ripple item was consulted for choreography. Actual cursor
effects come from Beam. This is a fictional editing demonstration, intentionally
silent; it does not record live editor interaction.
