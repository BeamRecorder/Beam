# Ai-Native

User requirements: a beautiful, exceptionally dynamic 15-second film; genuine Edits references and official logos; large zooms and continuous camera movement; no slideshow or feature-by-feature presentation. Sources must live at repository-root `examples/ai-edits/`, with `src/` and `references/`. Create the actual project with Beam CLI. Music and synchronized impacts, without voiceover, were explicitly selected.

Production decisions: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, English typography, offline bundled assets. The original 128 BPM score gives exactly 32 beats in 15 seconds. Genuine Instagram screenshots supply the interface and photographs; editorial typography sits outside the interface. No autonomous editing capability or growth result is claimed.

## Continuous shot

- 0–1.875s: readable, oversized “Your next idea.” next to the official Edits icon. Typography and camera are already moving.
- 1.875–3.75s: push through the icon into a fanned arrangement of the three official screens. “Edits meets AI.”
- 3.75–7.5s: the actual ideas conversation swings forward; push into its prompt. “What should I create next?”
- 7.5–9.375s: pan to the real profile review. “It knows your audience.”
- 9.375–11.25s: real project-gallery photographs sweep past the lens behind “Your ideas. Your vision.”
- 11.25–15s: pull through the photographs into the official identity. “Edits Assistant. More insight. Still your vision.” The final camera drift continues to the last frame.
