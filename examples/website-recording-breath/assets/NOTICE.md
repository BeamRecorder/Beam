# Asset provenance

- `taking-breath.webm`: supplied `gemini-generated-taking-breath.mp4`, adopted
  from the user's Downloads, compressed to VP9 without sound. AI-generated
  footage supplied by the user; no claim that this is a native capture.
- The build decodes the supplied clip to 240 local WebP frames at its original
  24 fps, scaled proportionally to the 396 × 704 preview.
- `taking-breath.webp`: its first frame, used only for the loop seam/poster.
- `figma-pointer.svg`: unchanged Beautiful Captures cursor artwork, reused
  from `examples/website-recorder-loop/assets/figma-pointer.svg`.
- Sonoma Horizon wallpaper and native RecorderBar components: existing Beam
  repository assets/components. Product sources are MPL-2.0.
- Hanken Grotesk: repository font, SIL OFL 1.1; license included alongside.
- The camera/audio video is the user's already edited
  `export-6s-woman-talking-facecam.webm`; its montage is preserved.
