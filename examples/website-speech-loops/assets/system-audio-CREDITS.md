## System audio waveform source

`system-audio.ogg` is [Empacotatron](https://opengameart.org/content/empacotatron),
by **Fupi**, published under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Copied from the locally frozen source in
`examples/ai-edits/references/audio/cc0/empacotatron_loop.ogg`.
The audio demo derives its system-track waveform from this file's decoded PCM.
Website loops are silent; the music is not mixed into their encoded output.
